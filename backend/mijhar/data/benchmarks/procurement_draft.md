# Draft award memo: Digital Services Contract (synthetic benchmark)

A SAR 2,000,000 contract was awarded directly to a single vendor without competitive tendering.
Evaluation criteria and weights were published before bids were opened.
Evaluators are not required to sign conflict-of-interest declarations.
Award decisions are published within 10 working days, followed by a 5 working day standstill period.
Procurement files are retained for 10 years.
